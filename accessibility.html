Accessibility statement

Last reviewed November 26, 2026

We build services that have to work for everyone, so this website should too. This statement covers dotmind.org.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site partially meets that standard: most of it does, with the known problems listed below.

What we’ve done

  • Text and links meet WCAG AA colour contrast.
  • Every image and diagram has a text alternative.
  • Pages use a logical heading order and are marked as English.
  • Text can be resized, and pages reflow on a phone-sized screen without sideways scrolling, except as noted below.

Known problems

  • Wide grids: the agent roles matrix and the example journey are large grids. On small screens you have to scroll sideways to read them. We plan to add a stacked, list-based version for small screens.
  • Diagram text: some diagrams in our Studio Studies posts contain labels and lists that aren’t all repeated in the text around them. We plan to add full text descriptions.

How we checked

We reviewed the site ourselves against WCAG 2.2 AA, including colour contrast, text alternatives, headings and layout on small screens. We haven’t yet had an independent audit or tested with a full range of assistive technology. We’ll update this statement when we do.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site doesn’t work for you, or you need information in a different format, email hello@dotmind.org. Tell us the page and what went wrong. We’ll reply within two working days.
