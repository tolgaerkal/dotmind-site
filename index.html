How it works

Every interaction follows the same arc.

Applying, reporting, requesting, booking or paying: the steps are the same. Agents do the legwork. People make the decisions.

  1. 01Resident

    Ask

    The resident explains what they need. The agent works out the right service and what it takes.

    Agent role: Guide
  2. 02Resident

    Provide

    Information and files are collected once, and checked on the spot.

    Agent role: Collect
  3. 03Agent

    Check

    Details are verified against records and rules, and the case goes to the right team.

    Agent role: Verify · Route
  4. 04Staff

    Decide

    Staff see a prepared summary with evidence and a suggested next step. They decide.

    Agent role: Prepare
  5. 05Resident

    Follow up

    Updates, outcomes, reasons and next steps, in plain language.

    Agent role: Communicate

Agent roles

Six roles. Built once, used everywhere.

Instead of a new agent for every service, we build six roles and set each one up with the rules, documents and systems of the service it supports. A seventh, Learn, works across all of them.

Guide

Understands what someone needs and explains the options and next steps.

For example: Which permit do I need? Am I likely eligible? What should I bring?

Hands off to a person when unsure

Collect

Gathers information and files once, and checks them at the source.

For example: Applications, complaint details, records requests, change-of-address forms

Helps fix problems; never turns people away

Verify

Checks identity, documents and facts against records and the rules.

For example: Proof of income, license history, inspection records, payment status

Shows the evidence for every finding

Prepare

Summarizes the case and drafts the next step for staff.

For example: Review summaries, recommendations, decision letters, information requests

A person makes every decision

Route

Sends work to the right team, agency or queue, with context attached.

For example: Cross-agency cases, urgent complaints, specialist reviews

Nothing falls between agencies

Communicate

Keeps people informed and explains outcomes clearly.

For example: Status updates, reminders, outcomes, reasons, how to appeal

Uses approved, plain-language messages

Across every role

Learn

Spots where people get stuck, quality issues and unusual patterns, and feeds what it finds back to the teams who run the service.

How we work with agencies

We start with one service and build with the people who use and run it.

  1. 01 · 2 weeks

    Discover

    Sit with residents and staff. Map the real journey and where people get stuck.

  2. 02 · 6 weeks

    Prototype

    Set up the agent roles on real (anonymized) cases and test with real users.

  3. 03 · 3 months

    Pilot

    Run alongside the current process with full human review, and measure.

  4. 04 · Ongoing

    Scale

    Roll out, reuse the roles for the next service, keep improving.

About the studio

Open by default. With you from start to finish.

Questions

Do agents make decisions about people?

No. Agents gather, check and prepare. Staff make every decision, and residents get clear reasons and a way to appeal.

Why roles instead of one agent per service?

The same few jobs happen in almost every interaction. Building them once means each new service is faster to launch and inherits tested safeguards.

What if someone can’t or won’t go online?

Phone, mail and in-person routes stay. Agents can also support staff handling those channels.

Where is residents’ information kept?

In the state’s own systems and cloud. Agents get access only to what each task needs, and every action is logged.

How is the studio funded?

Discovery is free. If the work goes ahead, the agency funds the prototype and pilot, with a fixed price agreed up front for each stage.
