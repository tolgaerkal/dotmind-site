Privacy notice

Last updated November 26, 2026

This notice explains what information this website, dotmind.org, collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. dotmind is responsible for it. Questions go to hello@dotmind.org.

It covers this website only. When we work with a government agency, residents’ data is handled under that agency’s own policies and our contract with it, not this notice.

What we collect

  • What you send by email to hello@dotmind.org: usually your name, work email, agency or team, and your message.
  • How the site is used: pages visited, how you arrived, rough location (city or region), and device and browser type, collected by Google Analytics using cookies.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider records basic request details, such as IP address and browser, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We don’t ask for, and don’t want, information about residents or cases through this website. Please don’t include it in your email.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk about working together.
  • To understand which pages are useful, so we can improve the site.
  • To keep the site running and secure.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t add you to a mailing list.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel hosts the site.
  • Our email provider stores the messages you send us.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, and serves the site’s fonts. Loading the fonts sends your IP address to Google.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own privacy and security terms.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: as long as we need to respond and follow up, then deleted. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.
  • Analytics: no longer than 14 months.
  • Hosting logs: for the short period our hosting provider keeps them.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. They don’t identify you by name. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works fully without them.

Your choices

Email hello@dotmind.org to ask for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. We’ll reply within two working days, and act on the request within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
